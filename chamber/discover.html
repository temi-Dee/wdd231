Discover Lagos

Lagos is Nigeria's largest city and the economic powerhouse of West Africa. With a population of over 15 million, it is a city of contrasts — from gleaming skyscrapers on Victoria Island to vibrant street markets on Lagos Island. Explore 8 must-see places below.

  • Population: 15+ million
  • Area: 1,171 km²
  • Major Industries: Finance, Technology, Oil & Gas, Trade, Entertainment
  • Founded: 1472 (Portuguese contact), Nigerian capital until 1991
