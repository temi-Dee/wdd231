NaijaEats

Individual Project Site Plan

Daniel Ayodele — WDD 231

1. Site Name

NaijaEats

NaijaEats is a food discovery and ordering platform focused on authentic Nigerian cuisine in Lagos. The name combines "Naija" — a popular, affectionate term for Nigeria — with "Eats", immediately communicating the site's purpose to both local and international audiences. It is short, memorable, and culturally resonant.

Optional domain: naijaeats.ng

2. Site Purpose

NaijaEats serves as a hub for food lovers in Lagos to discover top-rated local restaurants, explore menus featuring authentic Nigerian dishes, and place food orders online. The site will provide:

  • A searchable directory of Lagos restaurants and food vendors
  • Featured dish recommendations with photos and descriptions
  • A simple online order/reservation form for partner restaurants
  • A weekly "Dish of the Week" spotlight section
  • Contact and location information for each listed vendor

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent typical visitors to NaijaEats:

  1. "Where can I find the best jollof rice in Lagos Island?" — A local resident looking for a highly rated restaurant nearby.
  2. "Can I order egusi soup and pounded yam online for delivery to Victoria Island?" — A busy professional wanting to order authentic Nigerian food without leaving the office.
  3. "What Nigerian dishes should I try as a first-time visitor to Lagos?" — A tourist or expatriate exploring Nigerian cuisine for the first time.

4. Color Scheme

The color palette draws from the warm, vibrant tones of Nigerian street food culture.

#C0392B — Pepper Red

Primary brand color. Used for the header background, primary buttons, and key headings. Evokes the bold flavors of Nigerian pepper soup and suya.

#F39C12 — Jollof Gold

Accent color. Used for highlights, call-to-action hover states, badges, and section borders. Inspired by the golden color of jollof rice.

#1A1A2E — Deep Navy

Used for the footer background, navigation text, and body text on light backgrounds. Provides strong contrast and a modern feel.

#FDF6EC — Cream White

Page background color. Warm and inviting, reminiscent of parchment. Used as the main body background and card backgrounds.

5. Typography

Headings — Poppins (Bold 700, SemiBold 600)

Discover Authentic Lagos Flavours

Used for all page headings (h1–h3), the site name/logo text, and section titles. Poppins is modern, geometric, and highly legible at large sizes.

Body Text — Open Sans (Regular 400, SemiBold 600)

Order your favourite Nigerian dishes from the best restaurants in Lagos. Fast delivery, authentic taste, every time.

Used for paragraphs, labels, navigation links, and form elements. Open Sans is clean and highly readable at small sizes across all devices.
